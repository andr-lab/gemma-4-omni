## 1. Problem & Architectural Rationale
In real dialogue acceptance testing on RTX 3090, Turn 1 (long response) successfully plays back 55+ seconds of audio. During playout, 620 microphone frames were sent (frame ID 347 to 966), with frame 966 sent at `playout_completed_ns`.
Because the server finished synthesizing audio earlier while playback was still playing from the client FIFO, `response.unified_native_finalized` arrived at the client only 45 ms after `playout_completed_ns`.
The trailing 14 frames (frames 953-966) were sent in the final ~1000 ms of playback. Because the server processes frames at 80 ms ticks, their ACKs were in flight and arrived shortly after (at +200 to +500 ms).
However, `_long_response_input_window_metrics` immediately evaluated with `received_ns <= final_observation_ns`, prematurely cutting off these 14 valid ACKs and failing with:
`EvidenceError: long response input ACK ratio 97.742% is below 99.0% (606/620)`
