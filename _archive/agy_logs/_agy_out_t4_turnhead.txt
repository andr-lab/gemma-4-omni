#### 2.1. Specifiche Architetturali
- **Vettore di Input**: $z_t = [h_t^{\text{temp}}(2048) \,\|\, e_t^{\text{user}}(128) \,\|\, \Delta e_t^{\text{user}}(128)] \in \mathbb{R}^{2304}$.
- **Rete Neurale**: MLP causale Pre-LN a 3 stadi:
  $$\text{Linear}(2304, 1024) \to \text{LN} \to \text{SiLU} \to \text{Linear}(1024, 1024) \to \text{LN} \to \text{SiLU} \to \text{Linear}(1024, 256) \to \text{LN} \to \text{SiLU} \to \text{Linear}(256, 5)$$
  - **Parametri Trainable**: **3,678,213** (~3.68M).
- **Isolamento Rigoroso**: Backbone Gemma 4 E2B (4-bit) e `TemporalResidualAdapter` (59.55M) **congelati** (`requires_grad=False`, forward `no_grad()`).
- **Dataloader Bilanciato a 4 Bucket**: Partizionamento del dataset (*Monologue=743, Listen=832, Overlap=162, Mixed=36*) e campionamento batch bilanciato (**40% Monologue, 30% Listen, 30% Overlap**).
- **Loss**: Weighted Cross-Entropy ($w = [1.0, 5.0, 5.0, 3.0, 4.0]$) calcolata su tutti i frame attivi ($\text{UserActive} \lor \text{AgentActive}$).
